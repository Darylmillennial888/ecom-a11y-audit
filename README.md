## 👋 Welcome to ecom-a11y-audit

If you own a Shopify store, you want everyone to be able to use it. That includes people with visual, hearing, motor, or cognitive disabilities. Making your website accessible isn't just the right thing to do - it's also good for business and can protect you from legal issues.

**ecom-a11y-audit** is a tool that checks your Shopify store for accessibility problems. It tells you what's wrong, where the problem is (theme or app), and how to fix it. Think of it as a friendly inspector that walks through your store and gives you a clear report.

## 🎯 What This Tool Does

- **Scans your entire store** for common accessibility issues
- **Identifies whether problems come from your theme or from apps** you've installed
- **Gives you a prioritized list** so you know what to fix first
- **Follows WCAG 2.2 AA standards** - the global benchmark for web accessibility

## 📋 Understanding Your Report

After the scan completes, ecom-a11y-audit will show you a report on your screen and save it as a file. Here's what you'll see:

### 🔴 Priority 1 - High Impact
These are critical issues that prevent people from using your site. Examples include:
- Images without text descriptions
- Text that has too little contrast with the background
- Forms that can't be used with keyboard only

### 🟡 Priority 2 - Medium Impact
These issues make navigation difficult but don't completely block access. Examples include:
- Heading structure that's out of order
- Links that don't describe where they go
- Missing labels on form fields

### 🟢 Priority 3 - Low Impact
Small improvements that enhance the experience. Examples include:
- Redundant page titles
- Minor spacing issues
- Decorative elements that could confuse screen readers

## 🔧 Theme vs. App Attribution

One of the most helpful features of ecom-a11y-audit is telling you **where** the problem comes from.

- **Theme issues** - These come from your store's design template. You can fix them in your Shopify admin under **Online Store → Themes → Customize**, or by editing the theme code.
- **App issues** - These come from apps you've installed. You would need to contact the app developer, adjust app settings, or disable the app.

This saves you hours of trial and error figuring out what's causing the problem.

## 📝 How to Fix Common Issues

Here are simple fixes for the most common problems:

### Images Without Alt Text
- In Shopify admin, go to **Products → All products**
- Click on a product, scroll to the image section
- Add a brief description in the **Alt text** field (e.g., "Blue cotton t-shirt on white background")

### Low Contrast Text
- In theme settings, go to **Theme settings → Colors**
- Make sure your text color and background color are very different
- Use a contrast checker to verify

### Keyboard Navigation
- Test your site by pressing the Tab key repeatedly
- If you can't see where you are, your theme has a focus indicator issue
- In theme code, search for `:focus` and add styling to make it visible
